How to Use the System

A quick guide for first time users of the {{ BARANGAY_NAME }} Complaint System.

Step 1: Create your account

Click Register and fill in your name, email and password. We send a 6 digit code to your email. Enter it to verify your account, then log in.

Did not get the code? Check your spam folder, or use the resend button on the verification page. The code expires after 10 minutes.

Step 2: File your complaint

Go to File Complaint. Choose a category, write what happened, give the exact location, and attach a photo if you have one.

  • Say what the problem is and when it started.
  • Give a street, block or landmark so staff can find it.
  • A clear photo helps the barangay act faster.

Step 3: Keep your tracking code

After you submit, you get an 8 character tracking code, like ABC12345. We also email you a confirmation that the barangay received your complaint.

If you have an account, all your complaints are listed in My Complaints, so you will not lose them.

Step 4: Follow the progress

Type your code in the track box on the home page, or open the complaint from My Complaints. The status updates on its own while the page is open, and you get an email whenever it changes.

What each status means

Pending

Received and waiting for barangay staff to review it.

Under Review

Staff are checking the details, location and photo.

In Progress

Accepted, and the barangay is acting on it.

Resolved

Done. The barangay's notes appear on the tracking page.

Rejected

The barangay could not act on it. The reason appears on the tracking page.
